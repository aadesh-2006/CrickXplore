# CrickXplore

> *Explore the game beyond the scoreboard.*

CrickXplore is an experimental, frontend-heavy cricket universe blending Apple-level visual storytelling, digital museum archiving, and interactive collectibles. Designed for purists, strategists, and fans who revere the poetry, pressure, and tactile beauty of cricket.

---

## 🌟 Project Vision

Moving beyond generic scoreboard tables and SaaS dashboards, CrickXplore creates a cinematic sanctuary that celebrates cricket’s iconic eras, the physics behind immortal deliveries, legendary colosseums, and upcoming digital card battles.

---

## 🚀 Current Status: Phase 1 — Foundation & Landing Experience

- **Cinematic Hero**: Interactive 3D cricket relic responding to cursor physics, ambient illumination, and historical telemetry badges.
- **Universe Preview ("One Game. Infinite Stories.")**: Interactive showcase tiles for the 6 foundational realms with archival deep-dive preview modals.
- **Sensory Atmosphere Exhibition**: Breakdown of millisecond reaction times, English willow resonance, Duke ball reverse-swing aerodynamics, and morning toss stillness.
- **Web Audio Ambience**: Zero-dependency procedural stadium drone and acoustic willow strike audio synthesizer.
- **Responsive Architecture**: Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, laptop, and ultrawide displays.

---

## 🏛️ Planned Major Realms

1. **Players (The Pantheon)**: Deconstructed mastery, stance breakdowns, and historical impact matrices of cricket immortals.
2. **Moments (Living Memory)**: Narrative recreations, crowd decibel maps, and tension graphs of legendary matches.
3. **Stadiums (The Colosseums)**: Sacred grounds from Lord’s slope to the MCG roar, pitch geology, and microclimates.
4. **Timeline (Chronicles)**: 147-year journey from 1877 timeless tests to the modern white-ball franchise era.
5. **Cards (Digital Relics)**: Holographic digital collectible cards with dynamic foil reflections and tiered rarities.
6. **Card Game (Tactical Clash)**: Strategy arena pitting dream squads head-to-head with pitch and weather modifiers.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework**: React 19 + TypeScript
- **Bundler & Tooling**: Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4
- **Animations & Motion**: Framer Motion, GSAP
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Soundscape**: Web Audio API
